# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

In-house recruiters and hiring managers, treated as co-primary users. Recruiters do the setup work: defining job descriptions, uploading resume batches, and are the main audience for the per-JD chat. Hiring managers mostly consume the output: scanning ranked shortlists and drilling into why a candidate ranks where they do. Both need to move from a pile of resumes to a defensible shortlist quickly, and both need to be able to justify any decision the tool surfaces — to a candidate, to leadership, or to themselves — rather than trust a black-box number.

## Product Purpose

Turns unstructured resumes and job descriptions into ranked, evidence-backed shortlists. It exists because AI-assisted screening is only useful if the humans using it can verify it — every extracted field and every score component needs to trace back to something real in the source document. Success looks like: a recruiter uploads a batch of resumes and has a trustworthy, explainable shortlist in minutes, and a hiring manager can scan any candidate's ranking and understand exactly why without reading the raw resume.

## Positioning

The shortlisting tool that shows its work — every score traces back to a verbatim quote from the resume, not a black-box AI number.

## Brand Personality

Professional but warm: competent and calm rather than corporate-cold, approachable without being cutesy. It should feel like a well-crafted internal tool a recruiter is glad to open every day, not enterprise software they're forced to tolerate. Confidence is expressed through clarity and restraint, not through hype or aggressive visual energy.

## Anti-references

Must not read as a generic AI-dashboard template — no gradient text, no identical icon+heading card grids repeated as filler, no tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows stacked above every section. Must also not read as cold, dense enterprise ATS software in the Workday/Greenhouse mold — no gray utilitarian data-dump screens that bury the humans being evaluated under rows and filters.

## Design Principles

- Show the work: every score, every skill match, every extracted field is traceable to evidence; nothing is presented as a bare number with no source.
- Calm competence over hype: soft color and generous space signal trustworthiness and care, not excitement-chasing.
- Never hide the messy parts: Failed parses, excluded candidates, and low-confidence fields stay visible — greyed and flagged, never swept away or silently dropped.
- One tasteful animation beats five decorative ones: motion is used to clarify hierarchy and state, not to perform activity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Baseline WCAG AA contrast and full keyboard navigation. No additional accessibility requirements specified beyond that baseline.
